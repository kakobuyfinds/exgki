From discovery to decision / 02

How to use an ACBuy spreadsheet

An ACBuy spreadsheet organizes product references so you can compare options without losing the original listing. This page provides a searchable catalog snapshot rather than a downloadable Google Sheet. Start with a promising item, then confirm the source details and your chosen agent’s current purchasing rules.

Open the exact product

Follow the CAFANS product link and check the current title, available options and underlying seller information. Save the listing URL and selected variant; similar photographs can represent different products.

Compare fit before price

For clothing, compare chest width, length and shoulders against a garment you own. For footwear, review the supplied sizing guidance and distinguish foot length from insole length. Keep measurements in the same units.

Confirm buying and inspection options

Check whether your chosen agent accepts the item and source marketplace. Ask which QC photos or measurements are available, how extra requests are charged, and when return requests must be made.

Review the complete quote

Add domestic delivery, service charges and international shipping to the item price. Review destination restrictions, any applicable taxes and the shipping terms before you pay.

Useful answers / 04

ACBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is the ACBuy spreadsheet on this page?

It is an independent research page with six imported CAFANS catalog entries. You can search the visible shortlist, filter by category and sort estimated USD prices. The full catalog and current product details are available on CAFANS.

Is this the official ACBuy website?

No. This page is not operated by ACBuy and does not accept payments or manage orders. The ACBuy spreadsheet and Buy it now buttons both take you to CAFANS. Confirm which service will handle your purchase before entering payment details.

Are the prices and stock updated live?

No. The product names, images and estimated prices were imported from the CAFANS featured catalog. Prices, variants and availability can change. Review the current product page and your chosen service’s quote before ordering.

Do these products come with verified QC photos?

No inspection verification is claimed here. The displayed images are catalog photos. Ask the purchasing service about item-specific warehouse photos and measurements. Photos alone do not establish authenticity, material composition or long-term durability.

Can every listing be purchased through ACBuy?

Compatibility is not confirmed by this page. Agent policies and supported marketplaces can change. Check the exact source URL and item eligibility directly with your chosen agent before depositing money or placing an order.

How do I compare a hoodie with a T-shirt?

Compare measurements from the exact selected size rather than relying on S, M or L labels. Save chest width, body length, shoulder width and fabric information. Allow for how you intend to wear the garment, including layering.

Why does the final amount differ from a catalog price?

The catalog estimate covers the listed item reference. Domestic delivery, service fees, exchange-rate differences, international shipping and applicable destination charges may affect the amount you ultimately pay.
